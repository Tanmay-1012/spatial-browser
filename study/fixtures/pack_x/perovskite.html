Perovskite Crystal Stability and Degradation Mechanisms

Organic-inorganic halide perovskites exhibit exceptional optical absorption coefficients, but environmental longevity remains a primary engineering challenge. Intrinsic moisture sensitivity, ion migration under bias, and thermal phase segregation accelerate lattice decay.

Advanced encapsulation techniques employing cross-linked polyolefin elastomers and fluorinated barrier layers demonstrate substantial damp-heat test survival beyond 1,000 continuous hours at 85°C.

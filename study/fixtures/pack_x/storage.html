Thermal Energy Storage and Molten Salt Phase Transitions

Concentrating solar power (CSP) systems integrate two-tank sensible thermal storage using binary eutectic nitrate salts (60% NaNO3, 40% KNO3). Operating between 290°C and 565°C, these systems decouple thermal energy generation from instantaneous turbine dispatch.

Current research evaluates ternary chloride mixtures capable of withstanding operating temperatures exceeding 700°C for integration with supercritical carbon dioxide (sCO2) Brayton cycles.

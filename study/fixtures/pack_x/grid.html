Grid Interconnection Standards and Smart Inverter Control

High photovoltaic penetration challenges transmission stability through synthetic inertia deficits and steep evening ramp rates. Modern smart inverters compliant with IEEE 1547-2018 provide autonomous voltage support via volt-var and volt-watt curves.

Grid-forming inverters utilize droop control algorithms to establish voltage and frequency reference frames independently of synchronous rotating machinery.

Tandem Perovskite-Silicon Photovoltaic Cell Development

Monolithic two-terminal tandem architecture pairs a wide-bandgap metal-halide perovskite top subcell with an infrared-absorbing crystalline silicon bottom cell. By splitting the solar spectrum across two absorbers, thermalization losses are significantly mitigated compared to single-absorber systems.

Key Laboratory Benchmark: In November 2023, the certified laboratory record for tandem perovskite-silicon cell efficiency reached 24.4% under standard 1-sun test conditions, verified by international photovoltaic testing consortiums.

The top perovskite absorber absorbs high-energy blue and green photons, transmitting near-infrared wavelengths directly to the bottom silicon junction. Optical management requires sub-micron texturing and antireflective passivation coatings.

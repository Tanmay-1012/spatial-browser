Levelized Cost of Solar Electricity (LCOE) Financial Models

LCOE represents the net present value of total capital expenditure, operations, and maintenance divided by discounted lifetime electrical generation over an asset lifespan typically estimated at 25 to 30 years.

Utility-scale photovoltaic benchmarks in high-irradiance regions reflect levelized generation costs below $0.035 per kilowatt-hour, driven by balance-of-system efficiencies, single-axis tracker adoption, and module degradation rates under 0.5% annually.

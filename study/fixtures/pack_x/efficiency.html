Photovoltaic Spectral Response and Shockley-Queisser Limits

The thermodynamic Shockley-Queisser detailed balance limit dictates a maximum theoretical conversion efficiency of 33.7% for an unconcentrated, single p-n junction solar cell with an optimal bandgap of 1.34 eV.

Photons with energy below the semiconductor bandgap cannot produce electron-hole pairs, while photons with energy exceeding the bandgap lose excess kinetic energy through rapid thermal phonon emission.

Ancient Olympic Games and the Sacred Truce at Olympia

Dating traditionally from 776 BCE, the ancient Olympic Games were held quadriennially in the sanctuary of Zeus at Olympia in the Peloponnese. Competitors from across the Greek city-states gathered for religious rites and athletic contests.

The sacred Olympic Truce (Ekecheiria) prohibited military engagements and capital punishment across participating territories, guaranteeing safe passage for athletes, heralds, and pilgrims journeying to Olympia.

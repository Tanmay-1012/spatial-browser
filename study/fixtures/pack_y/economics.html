Host City Economic Impact and Long-Term Infrastructure Legacy

Economic analyses of recent Olympiads reveal consistent capital budget overruns exceeding 150%, with long-term financial returns heavily contingent on post-event municipal reuse of transit corridors and residential athlete villages.

The Montreal 1976 Games incurred substantial debt requiring thirty years to retire, whereas the Los Angeles 1984 Games capitalized on existing collegiate facilities and private corporate sponsorships to yield a operating surplus.

Origins of the Paralympic Movement and Stoke Mandeville Games

In July 1948, on the opening day of the London Olympic Games, neurologist Dr. Ludwig Guttmann organized an archery competition for sixteen paralyzed British World War II veterans at Stoke Mandeville Hospital in Buckinghamshire.

The event evolved into the International Stoke Mandeville Games, culminating in the first official Paralympic Games held in Rome in 1960 featuring 400 wheelchair athletes from 23 countries.

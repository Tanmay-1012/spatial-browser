Inaugural 1924 Winter Olympic Games at Chamonix

Originally designated the "International Winter Sports Week" organized by the French Olympic Committee under IOC patronage, the 1924 Chamonix contests were retroactively recognized as the first official Winter Olympic Games.

Over eleven days, 258 athletes from 16 nations competed across 16 events in bobsleigh, curling, ice hockey, figure skating, speed skating, and Nordic skiing beneath Mont Blanc.

Evolution of the Olympic Flame and Modern Torch Relay

While an Olympic flame burned at the 1928 Amsterdam and 1932 Los Angeles Games, the formal torch relay was instituted for the 1936 Berlin Olympiad. Lighted by solar rays using a parabolic mirror at Olympia, the flame was carried by over 3,000 runners across seven countries.

Modern protocols require the flame to maintain uninterrupted combustion from lighting at the Temple of Hera through arrival at the opening ceremony cauldron.
